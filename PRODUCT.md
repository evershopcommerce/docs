# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Store owners and small-business founders who want to sell online but do not want to run servers. Often not technical, often reading English as a second language. They ask: "Can someone just run EverShop for me?"

Secondary: developers and agencies who know EverShop and want to skip hosting work.

## Product Purpose

EverShop Cloud is the managed way to run EverShop: hosting, domain, email, backups, and one-click install of themes and extensions. One plan only. The /cloud page explains it and moves the visitor to the call to action. Success = the visitor knows what they get and clicks.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, plain-spoken. Same family as the EverShop site (green, Inter, light surfaces) but quieter and more spacious. Short sentences, concrete claims, no hype.

## Anti-references

- Purple-gradient SaaS landing pages
- Invented stats, uptime numbers, "lightning fast"
- Rows of identical icon cards
- Stock photos, urgency tricks, countdowns

## Design Principles

1. Say what you get, not how great we are.
2. Show the real mechanism (install flow, price curve) instead of decoration.
3. Stay in the EverShop family, but calmer.
4. Honest numbers only.
5. Write for second-language readers: short sentences, no idioms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: checked contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion respected, semantic headings, native `<details>` for the FAQ.
